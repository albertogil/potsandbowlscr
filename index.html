Pots & Bowls

Food made with care.

We offer balanced food, avoiding processed ingredients whenever possible, with healthy, simple and delicious options for different diets and restrictions.

From pesto and hummus to dressings, granola, plant milks, cold-pressed juices and desserts, much of what we serve is made from scratch for freshness and flavor.

We support responsible fishing, use cultivated shrimp, and proudly serve Café Willows from Hacienda Miramonte in Naranjo.
